# Code of Conduct

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, technical, and respectful.
- Focus review comments on correctness, maintainability, security, and operability.
- Keep disagreements evidence-based.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private tokens, credentials, or personal data.
- Deliberately disrupting project infrastructure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict access for behavior that harms the project or its users.
